## Fertility Treatment Cost Comparison in Lahore: IUI, IVF, ICSI, Tests & Hidden Charges

Fertility treatment costs in Lahore vary because the right treatment depends on the cause of infertility, male and female assessment findings, medicines, monitoring requirements, and whether advanced laboratory procedures such as ICSI, embryo freezing or genetic testing are needed.

This guide helps you compare **complete fertility treatment quotations, not just advertised package prices**. It explains the difference between IUI, IVF and ICSI cost structures, identifies common hidden charges, and gives you the exact questions to ask before starting treatment.

The World Health Organization defines infertility as the failure to achieve pregnancy after 12 months or more of regular unprotected sexual intercourse. It can involve male factors, female factors, both partners, or remain unexplained.

## IUI vs IVF vs ICSI: Cost Structure Comparison

IUI, IVF and ICSI are not interchangeable treatments. They are used for different clinical situations and involve different levels of monitoring, laboratory work, medication and cost.

| Cost component | IUI | IVF | ICSI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fertility consultation | Usually required | Usually required | Usually required |
| Fertility tests | Often required | Often required | Often required |
| Ovulation monitoring | Often required | Required | Required |
| Fertility medicines | May be required | Usually a major cost | Usually a major cost |
| Sperm preparation | Required | Required | Required |
| Egg retrieval | No | Yes | Yes |
| Laboratory fertilisation | No | Conventional IVF | Single-sperm injection |
| Embryo culture | No | Yes | Yes |
| Embryo transfer | No | Yes | Yes |
| Embryo freezing | Optional | Optional | Optional |
| PGT | Rarely applicable | Optional, case-specific | Optional, case-specific |
| General cost level | Lower | Higher | Higher, or additional to IVF |

IUI is usually the least expensive assisted conception option, but it is not automatically the best first step. IVF may be more appropriate for certain tubal, ovarian, age-related or unexplained fertility situations. ICSI is a laboratory technique used within IVF and may be considered when specific male-factor or fertilisation issues are present.

## What Affects Fertility Treatment Cost in Lahore?

### 1. Cause of infertility

The cause of infertility strongly affects the treatment pathway and total cost.

A couple with irregular ovulation may begin with assessment and ovulation support. A couple with severely low sperm count, previous fertilisation failure, blocked fallopian tubes or repeated unsuccessful treatment may require a different approach.

Cost may increase when treatment requires:

* Repeat semen analysis
* Hormonal testing
* Ultrasound monitoring
* [Ovulation induction](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/fertility-treatment/ovulation-induction)
* IUI cycles
* IVF
* ICSI
* Embryo freezing
* [Frozen embryo transfer](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/fertility-treatment/fet)
* [PGT](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/fertility-treatment/pgt)
* Donor treatment or surgical sperm retrieval, where clinically relevant

NICE guidance emphasises assessing possible causes of fertility problems and tailoring management to the individual clinical situation.

### 2. Male and female fertility assessment

Fertility difficulties can involve either partner. A [semen analysis](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/assessment/semen-analysis) is often an early male fertility investigation because it evaluates sperm concentration, motility, morphology and other semen characteristics.

NICE guidance recommends comparing semen analysis results with WHO reference values and considering further male assessment when results are abnormal.

### 3. Age, AMH and ovarian reserve

Age does not create a fixed price, but it can influence the treatment plan. Ovarian reserve testing, including AMH where clinically appropriate, may help guide treatment decisions.

A low AMH result does not automatically mean pregnancy is impossible or that IVF is necessary. AMH should be interpreted alongside age, menstrual history, ultrasound findings, previous ovarian response and reproductive goals.

If irregular ovulation or PCOS is part of the picture, see our guide to [PCOS and fertility](https://www.kamranfertility.com/resources/pcos-and-fertility) and the [ovulation induction and PCOS service](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/fertility-treatment/ovulation-induction).

### 4. Fertility medicines and injections

Medicines can be one of the largest variable costs in fertility treatment. Some clinics include them in an IVF package, while others bill them separately.

Ask whether the quotation includes:

* Ovarian stimulation injections
* Trigger medication
* Luteal-phase support
* Blood tests
* Ultrasound monitoring
* Medication adjustments if ovarian response is higher or lower than expected

Do not assume that an "IVF package" includes medicines. Always ask for a breakdown of medication costs, brands, doses and expected duration.

### 5. Number of cycles

Some patients conceive after IUI or one IVF cycle. Others require more than one attempt. Before starting treatment, ask the clinic to explain:

* What happens if the first cycle is unsuccessful?
* What are the options after a failed IUI or IVF cycle?
* Whether previous reports can reduce repeat testing?
* What clinical findings would change the treatment plan?
* What the realistic financial implications of multiple cycles may be?

No ethical fertility clinic can guarantee pregnancy or a live birth.

## IUI Cost in Lahore: What Should Be Included?

IUI, or intrauterine insemination, involves preparing a semen sample and placing selected sperm into the uterus around the time of ovulation. It is generally less complex than IVF because fertilisation occurs inside the body.

Public market references commonly place IUI at approximately **PKR 45,000–75,000 per cycle**, although the final cost depends on whether medicines, monitoring and sperm preparation are included.

Before choosing IUI, ask:

## IVF Cost in Lahore: What Should Be Included?

## ICSI Cost in Lahore: When Is It Needed?

ICSI is a specialised laboratory technique used as part of an IVF cycle. Instead of allowing sperm and eggs to fertilise together, a single sperm is injected directly into a mature egg.

ICSI may be considered in selected cases involving severe male-factor infertility, previous fertilisation difficulty or other specific clinical circumstances. It is not automatically necessary for every IVF patient.

Public market references commonly list ICSI around **PKR 750,000–850,000**, but the final cost depends on whether it is bundled with IVF, the medication protocol, and whether embryo freezing or genetic testing is added.

## How to Compare Fertility Clinic Quotations in Lahore

Use this 10-step checklist before choosing a clinic:

1. Ask each clinic for an itemised written estimate.
2. Mark every item as "included," "extra," or "not applicable."
3. Compare the total estimated cost of one complete cycle, not just the advertised package price.
4. Ask whether fertility medicines are included.
5. Ask how many ultrasound scans and blood tests are included.
6. Ask whether ICSI is included or charged separately.
7. Ask whether embryo freezing and annual storage are included.
8. Ask what happens if the cycle is cancelled before egg retrieval.
9. Ask whether previous reports can reduce repeat testing.
10. Ask what clinical findings would change the recommended treatment plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does fertility treatment cost in Lahore?

Fertility treatment costs vary by service. As indicative market references, IUI may cost around PKR 45,000–75,000 per cycle, ICSI around PKR 750,000–850,000, and IVF around PKR 830,000–970,000 per cycle, according to [Marham's price table](https://www.marham.pk/all-services/infertility-treatment/okara/area-okara). Medicines, monitoring, freezing and additional procedures can change the final amount.

### How much does IVF cost in Lahore?

Public market estimates commonly place IVF at approximately PKR 830,000–970,000 per cycle. Always confirm whether stimulation medicines, blood tests, ultrasound monitoring, embryo freezing and transfer are included.

### Is IUI cheaper than IVF?

Yes. IUI is generally less expensive and less complex than IVF because fertilisation occurs inside the body. However, IUI is not suitable for every fertility diagnosis, and IVF may be more appropriate in certain situations.

### Is ICSI more expensive than IVF?

ICSI can be priced separately from IVF or included in an IVF package. It may be recommended when severe male-factor infertility or previous fertilisation problems are present, but it is not automatically necessary for every IVF cycle.

### Does the IVF price include medicines?

Not always. Some clinics include medicines, while others charge them separately. Always request an itemised quotation that clearly separates consultation, investigations, injections, procedures, laboratory fees, freezing and follow-up.

### What is included in an IVF package?

It depends on the clinic. A package may include ovarian stimulation monitoring, egg retrieval, laboratory fertilisation, embryo culture and embryo transfer. Medicines, ICSI, freezing, PGT and frozen embryo transfer may be additional.

### Are fertility medicines expensive?

They can be a significant part of the total cost. The amount depends on the stimulation protocol, dosage, brand, duration of treatment and how the ovaries respond.

### How much does a fertility consultation cost in Lahore?

Public references indicate consultation fees in Pakistan may range from around PKR 1,000 to PKR 5,000, depending on the specialist and clinic. Confirm the consultation fee, duration, and whether follow-up visits are charged separately.

### Do I need IVF if I have PCOS?

No. Many women with PCOS can conceive naturally or with targeted support such as [ovulation induction](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/fertility-treatment/ovulation-induction). IVF may be considered in selected cases, particularly when other treatments have not been successful or when additional fertility factors are present. Read more about [PCOS and fertility](https://www.kamranfertility.com/resources/pcos-and-fertility).

### Does low AMH mean IVF is necessary?

No. Low AMH may indicate reduced ovarian reserve, but it does not automatically mean IVF is required or that natural conception is impossible. AMH should be interpreted alongside age, ultrasound findings, menstrual history and previous treatment response.

### Is semen analysis necessary before fertility treatment?

Yes, [semen analysis](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/assessment/semen-analysis) is commonly an early part of fertility assessment because male factors contribute significantly to infertility. NICE guidance recommends comparing semen analysis with WHO reference values and considering further assessment if results are abnormal.

### Can infertility affect men as well as women?

Yes. Infertility may involve male factors, female factors, both partners, or unexplained causes. A comprehensive assessment considers both partners where clinically appropriate.

### When should we see a fertility specialist?

Assessment is often recommended after 12 months of trying to conceive without success when the female partner is under 35, and after six months when she is 35 or older. Earlier consultation may be appropriate if there is a known reproductive health concern.

### Can previous fertility reports reduce treatment costs?

Yes. Previous semen analyses, hormonal tests, ultrasound reports and treatment records can help the specialist avoid unnecessary repeat investigations, provided the reports are recent and clinically relevant.
