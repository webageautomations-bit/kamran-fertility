## What Is IUI and Who Needs It?

**IUI (intrauterine insemination) is a fertility treatment where washed sperm is placed directly into the uterus around ovulation.** It is less invasive and cheaper than IVF.

IUI is commonly recommended for couples with:

* Mild male-factor infertility (see [male infertility treatment](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/fertility-treatment/male-infertility))
* Unexplained infertility
* Cervical factor issues
* Ovulation problems, often combined with [ovulation induction](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/fertility-treatment/ovulation-induction)

## How Much Does IUI Cost in Pakistan?

**Expect roughly PKR 20,000–60,000 per cycle in major private clinics.** Public hospitals may charge less (around PKR 10,000–20,000), but exact fees should always be confirmed.

Rates are usually lower in smaller cities and public hospitals, and higher in top private centres. The total depends on which components are included:

* Consultation
* Baseline tests
* Semen analysis
* Ovulation drugs
* Monitoring scans
* Sperm preparation (lab fee)
* The IUI procedure itself
* Post-IUI medication

**Illustrative example:** a couple might pay about PKR 5,000 for the initial consultation, PKR 7,000 for baseline tests, PKR 3,000 for semen analysis, PKR 10,000 for drugs and monitoring, and PKR 5,000 for the procedure, a total of roughly PKR 30,000.

**Key takeaway:** Plan for more than one cycle. Most pregnancies from IUI occur within 2–3 cycles, so budget at least PKR 50,000–100,000 in case multiple rounds are needed. Always ask for an itemized quote.

## Factors Influencing IUI Cost

**The main factors are the clinic (public or private), the city, the treatment protocol (natural or medicated cycle) and the level of monitoring.**

* **Clinic and location:** Premium clinics in big cities charge more.
* **Medication protocol:** Natural cycles cost less, while injectables add thousands of rupees. Oral drugs such as clomiphene or letrozole are cheap (around PKR 500 per tablet), whereas gonadotropin injections can cost PKR 5,000–10,000 per cycle. Read more about [ovulation induction and PCOS care](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/fertility-treatment/ovulation-induction).
* **Monitoring intensity:** More ultrasounds and blood tests mean higher fees.
* **Donor sperm:** If needed, extra screening and donor fees apply.
* **Repeat cycles:** The total multiplies with each attempt.
* **Currency movements:** Exchange rates can affect the price of imported drugs.

**Key takeaway:** Personalized plans cause wide cost swings. Ask your doctor whether a simpler protocol is both cheaper and effective for you.

## Success Rates of IUI

**IUI success is modest: roughly 10–25% per cycle, depending on age and fertility factors.**

* Women under 35: about 20–25% per cycle
* Women 35–40: about 10–15%
* Women over 40: about 5%
* Cumulative success after 3 cycles can reach 40–60% for good candidates

Odds are lower when there is tubal damage or severe male infertility, in which case IVF is usually advised. Age, sperm quality, ovulation function and uterine health all matter. A [female fertility assessment](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/assessment/female-fertility-assessment) and [male fertility assessment](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/assessment/male-fertility-test) help show whether IUI is a good fit.

**Key takeaway:** Plan realistically. Many couples need 2–3 cycles. If IUI is unsuccessful after a few tries, consider IVF.

## Treatment Pathway and Timeline

**A typical IUI cycle takes about 2–4 weeks from the first consultation to the pregnancy test.**

1. **Week 1 – Consultation and tests.** Consultation (day 1), baseline tests (days 2–5), medication starts (days 3–5).
2. **Week 2 – Ovulation induction and monitoring.** Follicle ultrasounds around days 10–13. See [ovulation assessment](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/assessment/ovulation-test).
3. **Week 3 – Trigger and insemination.** hCG trigger around day 14, then IUI about 36 hours later.
4. **Week 4 – Two-week wait.** Blood pregnancy test 10–14 days after IUI.

**Key takeaway:** Be prepared for about a month per cycle and plan time off work around the procedure and scans.

## Contraindications and Risks

**IUI is not suitable for everyone.** It is unlikely to work when both fallopian tubes are blocked or male infertility is severe.

* **Contraindications:** fully blocked tubes (checked with HSG), severe endometriosis or fibroids, and severe male factor infertility.
* **Risks:** multiple pregnancy (if many follicles develop), mild cramping or spotting, very rare infection, and ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome (OHSS) with aggressive drug use.

**Key takeaway:** You will be screened first to confirm IUI is appropriate. Discuss risks and alternatives with your doctor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is intrauterine insemination (IUI)?

A fertility procedure where washed sperm is placed directly into the uterus around ovulation. It moves sperm closer to the egg and is often combined with fertility drugs to time ovulation. Recovery is quick and no anesthesia is needed.

### How much does an IUI cycle cost in Karachi or Lahore?

Typically PKR 30,000–60,000 in top private clinics, and possibly PKR 10,000–25,000 in public hospitals. Costs include consultations, tests, medication and the procedure. Always get an itemized quote.

### Does insurance cover IUI in Pakistan?

Rarely. Most plans do not cover fertility treatment, so costs are usually out of pocket. Some charities and hospitals offer discounted programs for low-income couples.

### What factors affect IUI success rates?

Age is the biggest factor: about 20% per cycle under 35, dropping to around 5% by 40. Sperm quality, ovulation health, uterine conditions, BMI and smoking also play a role.

### Can I do IUI at home?

No. IUI should be done in a clinic under medical supervision. Home kits do not replace proper sperm washing, timing and sterile technique.

### Is IUI painful?

Most women report minimal discomfort. It feels like mild pressure or a pinch. Cramping or spotting for a day or two afterward is common.

### How many IUI cycles are needed?

Many couples try 2–3 cycles. Since per-cycle success is around 10–20%, clinics often recommend up to three attempts before re-evaluating.

### How long until I know if I am pregnant?

About two weeks after the procedure. A blood beta-hCG test is done 10–14 days post-IUI. Early symptoms such as cramping or spotting are not conclusive.

### What are common mistakes after IUI?

Ignoring medical instructions, such as skipping prescribed progesterone or follow-up visits. Avoid heavy lifting, extreme exercise and alcohol during the wait.

### If IUI fails, what next?

Consider IVF. After 2–3 unsuccessful cycles, specialists often recommend [IVF](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/fertility-treatment/ivf) or [ICSI](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/fertility-treatment/icsi), which cost more but have higher success rates.
