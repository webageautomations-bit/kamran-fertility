## IVF Success Rate in Pakistan (2026): Live Birth Rates by Age and What the Evidence Says

By **[Dr. Kamran Saleem](https://www.kamranfertility.com/dr-kamran-saleem)**, Kamran Fertility Clinic, Lahore | Last reviewed: November 2026

If you are thinking about IVF, your first question is probably, “What are our real chances?” The honest answer is that the IVF success rate in Pakistan depends mostly on the woman’s age, her ovarian reserve, the man’s sperm quality and the standard of the clinic and laboratory. Published clinic-level outcome data from Pakistan is also limited, so it helps to read local studies alongside international benchmarks. This guide explains what the evidence shows, how to read success statistics, and which popular claims do not hold up. ## What Pakistani Studies Say About IVF Success Rates

The most cited peer-reviewed Pakistani figures come from a [Journal of the College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan study (2004)](https://ecommons.aku.edu/pakistan_fhs_mc_women_childhealth_obstet_gynaecol/103). Researchers from Aga Khan University followed 310 couples who had 330 IVF or ICSI cycles at one Karachi centre between July 2000 and December 2002.

* The overall pregnancy rate was 35.2%.
* The take-home live birth rate was 25.1%.
* Cycles done for male-factor infertility alone had a higher chance of success than cycles for female-factor or unexplained infertility.

The authors judged their results comparable to international reports, but also said Pakistan needs more data from its centres. That caution still applies. This was one clinic, more than twenty years ago, before many of today’s laboratory techniques, so treat it as a historical reference point rather than a prediction.

## IVF Success Rate by Age: An International Benchmark

Female age is the strongest predictor of IVF success when you use your own eggs. Because we cannot cite widely published national figures by age band for Pakistan, the table below uses the UK regulator’s data (HFEA) as an international benchmark.

| Age of woman | Live birth rate per embryo transferred (UK, 2022, preliminary) | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 18–34 | 35% | Highest success rates |
| 35–37 | 26% | Decline begins |
| 38–39 | 18% | Further decline |
| 40–42 | 10% | Possible, but much less likely with own eggs |
| 43–44 | 5% | Very low with own eggs |

Source: [HFEA, Fertility treatment 2022: preliminary trends and figures](https://www.hfea.gov.uk/about-us/publications/research-and-data/fertility-treatment-2022-preliminary-trends-and-figures/). Fresh embryo transfers using the patient’s own eggs; birth rates for recent years are preliminary.

These are UK averages per embryo transferred. They are not a forecast for Pakistan or for you. A cycle can also stop before transfer, for example if too few eggs are retrieved, so a rate counted per started cycle is usually lower than a rate counted per transfer. The HFEA also reports that live birth rates per embryo transferred stayed above 20% on average for each of the first three IVF cycles ([2018 data](https://hfea.gov.uk/4446.aspx)).

## Pregnancy Rate vs Live Birth Rate: How to Read Success Statistics

Clinics report success in different ways, and the numbers are not interchangeable.

1. Biochemical pregnancy (positive hCG test): a blood test about two weeks after transfer. It can include very early losses.
2. Clinical pregnancy: an ultrasound shows a gestational sac and, usually, a heartbeat at around 6 to 7 weeks.
3. Live birth rate: the percentage of treatments that end in a baby. This is the figure that matters most.

![Difference between positive pregnancy test, clinical pregnancy and live birth in IVF](/assets/ivf-pregnancy-vs-live-birth.jpg)

The gap is real. [In the same UK data](https://www.hfea.gov.uk/about-us/publications/research-and-data/fertility-treatment-2022-preliminary-trends-and-figures/), women aged 18 to 34 had a pregnancy rate of 42% per embryo transferred but a live birth rate of 35%. Always ask which of the three a clinic is quoting.

## Why IVF Clinic Success Rates in Pakistan Are Hard to Compare

Two clinics can report very different rates without one being better. [Dawn’s reporting on Pakistani IVF centres](https://www.dawn.com/news/734355) noted that some centres show very good rates partly because they set an age cut-off and do not treat older patients. Rates also depend on the denominator (per cycle, per transfer, per patient), the number of cases behind the figure, and whether frozen transfers are included.

Questions worth asking any clinic:

* What is your live birth rate per embryo transfer for my age group, and how many transfers is it based on?
* Which years do the figures cover, and are fresh and frozen transfers counted together?
* Do you exclude older patients or complex cases from the figures?
* How many embryos do you usually transfer? Multiple pregnancies carry higher risks, and the UK’s IVF multiple-birth rate fell from 28% in the 1990s to 4% in 2022 ([HFEA, 2022 report above](https://www.hfea.gov.uk/about-us/publications/research-and-data/fertility-treatment-2022-preliminary-trends-and-figures/)).
* Who performs the retrieval and transfer, and who leads the embryology laboratory?

## What Affects Your Chances of IVF Success?

### Age and ovarian reserve

Age affects both the number and the genetic quality of eggs. [Tests such as AMH and antral follicle count](https://asrm.org/practice-guidance/practice-committee-documents/testing-and-interpreting-measures-of-ovarian-reserve-a-committee-opinion-2020) estimate how many eggs may respond to stimulation, but they do not measure egg quality directly. See our **[hormonal analysis](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/assessment/hormonal-analysis)** and **[female fertility assessment](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/assessment/female-fertility-assessment)** pages to learn how we approach these tests.

### Embryo quality

Not every fertilised egg becomes a healthy embryo. Embryologists grade embryos and may grow them to the blastocyst stage when enough are available, which helps them choose the most promising one for transfer.

### Sperm quality

Sperm contribute half of the embryo’s genetic material. Count, movement and shape are assessed in a semen analysis, and any IVF plan should start with one. Learn about our **[semen analysis](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/assessment/semen-analysis)** and **[male fertility assessment](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/assessment/male-fertility-test)** services.

### The uterus

Fibroids, polyps or scar tissue inside the uterus can interfere with implantation. They are usually checked by ultrasound before treatment and managed first when needed.

### Laboratory and protocol

Medicine doses and timing should be adjusted to your hormone profile and response, and results depend on the skill of the embryology team. This is why age-specific outcome data matters when you choose a clinic.

## Does ICSI Improve IVF Success?

ICSI (injecting a single sperm directly into an egg) was developed for male-factor infertility and for couples whose eggs failed to fertilise in a previous cycle. For couples without a male factor, the evidence is different. The American Society for Reproductive Medicine’s [2026 committee opinion on ICSI](https://asrm.org/practice-guidance/practice-committee-documents/intracytoplasmic-sperm-injection-for-nonmale-factor-indications-a-committee-opinion-2026/) concluded that routine ICSI in couples without male-factor infertility may offer no benefit, and it notes that two large population studies linked it with worse outcomes. In short, ICSI helps when sperm is the problem; it is not automatically better for everyone. ## Fresh vs Frozen Embryo Transfer: Which Has the Better Success Rate?

[A Cochrane review of 8 randomised trials (4,712 women)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33539543/) found probably little or no difference in cumulative live birth between a freeze-all strategy and conventional IVF with a fresh transfer. Freezing all embryos may lower the risk of ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome (OHSS) in women who are at risk, but it delays the transfer. So frozen transfer is not automatically better. The right choice depends on your response to stimulation and your OHSS risk. ## What Helps, and What Does Not, Before and During IVF

* **Get both partners assessed first.** Ovarian reserve tests, a pelvic ultrasound and a semen analysis shape the treatment plan. See our **[female fertility assessment](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/assessment/female-fertility-assessment)** and **[male fertility assessment](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/assessment/male-fertility-test)** pages.
* **Be cautious with supplements.** [A Cochrane review of antioxidants for women](https://www.cochrane.org/CD007807/) found very low-quality evidence and says it is uncertain whether they improve live birth. For men, [a 2022 Cochrane review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35506389/) found antioxidants may raise live birth rates, but with very low certainty and no clear benefit once higher-risk studies were removed. Ask your doctor before buying supplements.
* **Skip bed rest after transfer.** [A 2022 meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35621274/) found no evidence to recommend it, and [a larger review in Human Reproduction Update](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35325124/) linked it to a lower clinical pregnancy rate.
* **Question add-ons.** [The HFEA rates PGT-A embryo testing](https://hfea.gov.uk/treatments/treatment-add-ons/pre-implantation-genetic-testing-for-aneuploidy-pgt-a/) as not shown to improve the chance of a baby for most patients, although it may reduce miscarriage risk. Ask what evidence supports any extra you are offered, and see our **[embryo genetic testing (PGT)](https://www.kamranfertility.com/services/fertility-treatment/pgt)** page.
* **Look after your emotional health.** [The WHO notes](https://www.who.int/news/item/04-04-2023-1-in-6-people-globally-affected-by-infertility) that infertility can cause significant distress and stigma. Counselling and support are part of good care.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a good IVF success rate in Pakistan?

A good IVF success rate is the live birth rate per embryo transfer for your age group, not a headline pregnancy rate. [One older Karachi study (2000-2002)](https://ecommons.aku.edu/pakistan_fhs_mc_women_childhealth_obstet_gynaecol/103) reported a 25.1% live birth rate overall, while [UK regulator data](https://www.hfea.gov.uk/about-us/publications/research-and-data/fertility-treatment-2022-preliminary-trends-and-figures/) show 35% for women aged 18 to 34. Ask any clinic for age-specific live birth figures and the number of cycles behind them.

### How many IVF cycles do most couples need?

There is no fixed number, because it depends on age, ovarian reserve and embryo quality. [UK regulator data from 2018](https://hfea.gov.uk/4446.aspx) showed live birth rates per embryo transferred stayed above 20% on average across the first three IVF cycles, then fell in later cycles when patients were also older. Your doctor can estimate how many attempts are realistic after your test results.

### Can women over 40 conceive with IVF?

Yes, but live birth chances with your own eggs are much lower. [Preliminary UK regulator figures for 2022](https://www.hfea.gov.uk/about-us/publications/research-and-data/fertility-treatment-2022-preliminary-trends-and-figures/) show about 10% per embryo transferred at ages 40 to 42 and about 5% at 43 to 44. Ovarian reserve and embryo quality vary widely, so a personal assessment tells you more than any average.

### Does bed rest after embryo transfer improve IVF success?

No. Systematic reviews of randomised trials found no benefit from bed rest after embryo transfer, and [a 2022 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35325124/) linked it to a lower clinical pregnancy rate. Early mobilisation did not harm outcomes in the studies reviewed. Ask your clinic what level of activity it advises.

### Do antioxidant supplements improve IVF success?

The evidence is limited. Cochrane reviews of antioxidant supplements [in women](https://www.cochrane.org/CD007807/) and [in men](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35506389/) found only low or very low certainty evidence of benefit for live birth, and the male result weakened when higher-risk studies were removed. Some supplements can cause mild stomach upset, so ask your doctor before starting any.

### When should we see a doctor about not conceiving?

[Infertility is usually defined](https://www.who.int/news/item/04-02-2020-multiple-definitions-of-infertility) as not conceiving after 12 months of regular unprotected intercourse, so that is the usual point to seek assessment. Many doctors advise earlier review if the woman is older or either partner has a known condition, because age affects IVF outcomes. Both partners should be tested, including a semen analysis.
